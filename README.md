### 🔬 Featured Research Preprint & Clinical AI

- **Preprint:** *DiaGuard: An Explainable Multi-Modal Deep Learning Framework for Joint Diabetic Retinopathy and Foot Ulcer Risk Screening* (2026)
- **Permanent DOI:** [`10.5281/zenodo.24419798`](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24419798) | **Record:** [zenodo.org/records/24419798](https://zenodo.org/records/24419798)
- **Research Highlights:**
  - Unifies **5-stage Diabetic Retinopathy (DR)** grading (`ResNet-50`) and **binary Diabetic Foot Ulcer (DFU)** screening (`EfficientNet-V2-S`) with real-time **Grad-CAM** spatial attention overlays and max-weighted microvascular risk fusion.
  - Achieves **0.9985 ROC-AUC**, **98.06% Sensitivity**, and **99.07% Specificity** ($n = 211$ validation scans) using photometric-safe color augmentation ($\Delta H \le 0.03$) and class-weighted Focal Loss ($\gamma = 2.0$).
  - Deployed as an end-to-end **FastAPI** inference engine and responsive web triage portal (~2.4 s dual-scan CPU runtime).

#### PhD Research Portfolio (4-Stage Progression)
1. **Stage 1 — [`Clinical Image Preprocessing & Photometric Standardization`](https://github.com/niralivachhani26/diabetic-core-ai-pipeline):** Retinal fundus and plantar dermal preprocessing pipelines designed to preserve diagnostic erythema and microvascular biomarkers.
2. **Stage 2 — [`Ocular Diabetic Retinopathy Grading (`ResNet-50`)`](https://github.com/niralivachhani26/diabetic-core-ai-pipeline-task2):** Transfer learning architecture for 5-stage International Clinical DR severity classification (`Grade 0–4`).
3. **Stage 3 — [`Plantar Diabetic Foot Ulcer Detection & Grad-CAM (`EfficientNet-V2-S`)`](https://github.com/niralivachhani26/diabetic-dfu-xai-pipeline-task3):** Hard-margin ulcer detection optimized with Focal Loss and spatial explainability verification.
4. **Stage 4 — [`DiaGuard Multi-Modal Clinical Portal`](https://github.com/niralivachhani26/Practical-4-diaguard-portal):** Unified dual-branch inference engine, real-time Grad-CAM hook integration, and interactive microvascular risk triage dashboard.
